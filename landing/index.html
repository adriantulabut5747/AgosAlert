01 How it works

From the rain forecast to help at your door.

What AgosAlert does behind the screen, step by step. The numbers in the first two cards are today's, live.

  1. Step 01

    We read the sky

    Every 15 minutes, AgosAlert asks Open-Meteo for Mabalacat's forecast: the chance of rain today, how many millimeters are expected, and the weather code, which tells a light shower from a thunderstorm.

  2. Step 02

    Rain becomes a risk level

    The app checks those numbers against three rules, from worst to best. The first rule that matches is the flood outlook you see on the Home tab. It's an estimate, not an official warning.

    High30 mm or more, or a thunderstorm with a 60%+ chance
    Moderate10 mm or more, or a 70%+ chance of rain
    LowAnything less

  3. Step 03

    Neighbors report the water

    Anyone logged in can report a flood: pick the barangay, add a photo, and set how deep the water is, from ankle-deep to over the head. The report becomes a pin on the map, and other people vote on whether it's accurate.

    1. photo_cameraReportedDay 0
    2. location_onPinnedOn the map
    3. thumb_upVoted onAccurate?
    4. deleteRemovedDay 14

    Reports in the app are sample data for now.

  4. Step 04

    You get help

    The Assistance tab keeps help one tap away. Press and hold SOS to call for help or request a rescue, find the nearest open evacuation center with directions, or call a hotline.

04 FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is AgosAlert official?

No. AgosAlert is a school capstone project made by BSIT students. It isn't made by, or connected to, the Mabalacat City government. For official warnings, follow PAGASA and the Mabalacat CDRRMO.

Is the data real?

The weather and the flood outlook are live. They come from Open-Meteo and update every 15 minutes. Flood reports, alerts, and evacuation centers are sample data for now, marked SAMPLE in the app, until the app is connected to a real server.

Why isn't it on Google Play or the App Store?

AgosAlert is a web app for now, so it works in any browser without installing anything. Mobile versions are planned. Until then, you can add it to your home screen: in Chrome on Android, open the menu and tap Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
