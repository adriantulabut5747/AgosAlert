Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Your account is your name, email and barangay. Your password goes to our login provider, who keeps it scrambled (hashed). We never see it.
  • A flood report you post (the spot, photo, note and your name) is public for 14 days, then deleted.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We don't sell your data.
  • You can change your name or delete your account at any time, in the app.

Who we are

AgosAlert is a flood-watch app for Mabalacat City, Pampanga, made by BSIT students as a school capstone project. It is an unofficial demo, not an app of the Mabalacat City government. The student team decides what the app collects and why, so under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) we are the personal information controller.

Contact for anything on this page: wildparchrome@gmail.com.

We use these under Section 12 of the Data Privacy Act: your account data because it's needed for the service you signed up for; your reports and votes because you chose to post them; and keeping the app safe (moderation, stopping abuse) as our legitimate interest.

Kept on your own device, not sent to us

  • Your login session, so you stay logged in after a reload.
  • Your go-bag checklist ticks (Help tab).
  • The number and phone of the last rescue request sent from this device, so it can show you its status.

These live in your browser's storage (or the phone app's storage). Logging out or clearing the site's data removes them. We don't use cookies for tracking.

Your location

The app only asks for your device's location when you tap "Use my location" in the report form or "Share my location" in a rescue request. It saves only the pin you post, or the location sent with your request. The weather is always for Mabalacat City, never for your position. In the web app, a photo's hidden details (EXIF data, including any GPS position) are removed before it's uploaded.

Things that stay with you

Calling a hotline or SOS opens your phone's own dialer, and the "I'm safe" message goes through your device's share menu to whoever you pick. We don't receive either.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no selling or renting of your data.
  • We don't ask for your phone number, birthday, or government ID.
  • Other users can't see your email.

Services we use

  • Supabase runs our database, login and photo storage, and processes data only for us. Its servers are in Singapore (Southeast Asia), outside the Philippines. We stay responsible for your data wherever it's stored, as Section 21 of the Data Privacy Act requires.
  • Gmail (Google) sends our emails, like password reset links, so Google handles your email address and the message.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the web app. Like any web host, its servers see your IP address when you visit.
  • Open-Meteo, with MET Norway and wttr.in as backups, supplies the weather. Your browser asks them for Mabalacat's weather, so they see your IP address, but not your account or location.
  • Google Fonts supplies the font of the landing page (not the app), so Google sees your IP address when it loads.
  • Google Maps opens only if you tap "Directions". Google's own privacy policy applies there.

The map, its code and the 3D Tugak all load from our own site, not from outside map services.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this page);
  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it (you can change your name and barangay in the app);
  • object to how it's used;
  • erase or block it (see Delete your account);
  • data portability: get your data in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • damages, if you're harmed by wrong or unlawful use of your data;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these, email wildparchrome@gmail.com from the address on your account, so we know the request is really yours.

Delete your account

In the app: avatar (top right) → Settings → Delete account → confirm. This deletes your account, your flood reports and their photos, your votes, your rescue requests and your read marks right away. It can't be undone.

Without the app: email wildparchrome@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my AgosAlert account". We'll delete the same data and reply when it's done.

One exception: when a report expires on its own after 14 days, its photo file can stay in storage until we clean up. Deleting a report yourself, or deleting your account, removes its photos at once.

How we protect it

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Database rules (row-level security) decide what each person can read or change, so the app can't be tricked into showing someone else's email.
  • Passwords are hashed by our login provider. Nobody on the team can read them.

If a data breach ever puts your information at risk, we'll tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of finding out, as NPC Circular 16-03 requires.

Children

Guests can use AgosAlert without an account. If you're under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you make an account or post a report. If you think a child made an account without that, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this page

If we change what we collect or why, we'll update this page and its date first. If a change is big, the app will say so before it applies to you.
