1. Using AgosAlert

By using AgosAlert (the app and this website) you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it. Our Privacy Policy explains what we do with your data.

2. What AgosAlert is

AgosAlert is a flood-watch app for Mabalacat City, Pampanga, made by BSIT students as a school capstone project. It is an unofficial demo: it is not run or endorsed by the Mabalacat City government, the CDRRMO, PAGASA or the NDRRMC. Who will run it after the project is not decided yet. It may change or stop at any time.

3. Not an emergency service

If you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local rescue hotline right away. Don't wait for the app.

Always follow evacuation orders and warnings from official sources: the city government, the CDRRMO, your barangay, PAGASA and the NDRRMC. AgosAlert doesn't replace them, and no one watches the app around the clock.

4. The information can be wrong

  • Weather comes from forecast models (Open-Meteo and backups), not from a station in your street.
  • The flood outlook is our own estimate from the rain forecast, not an official warning.
  • Flood reports are posted by users. A report is unchecked unless it's marked as verified.
  • Anything marked SAMPLE is made-up demo data.

Use AgosAlert as one source among others, and check with official sources before acting on it.

5. Your account

  • One account per person. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what's posted from your account.
  • Use your name or a nickname. Don't pretend to be another person, a government office, a rescuer or an official.
  • If you're under 18, use an account only with a parent's or guardian's OK.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings (see the Privacy Policy).

6. Rules for flood reports

People may decide where to go based on what you post, so:

  • Report only what you saw yourself, at the place you pin, and recently.
  • Post only photos you took, or have the owner's permission to share.
  • Protect other people's privacy: no close-up faces, plate numbers, IDs, house numbers, or the inside of someone's home. Don't post other people's names or contact details in notes.
  • No insults, hate, threats, nudity, violence, ads or spam.
  • No fake, prank or exaggerated reports. They can send people into danger or away from safety. Spreading false news can also be a crime under Article 154 of the Revised Penal Code (as amended by RA 10951), with heavier penalties when done online under the Cybercrime Prevention Act (RA 10175).

Vote honestly: thumbs up means the report looks right to you, thumbs down means it looks wrong. You can't vote on your own reports.

7. What you post

You keep the rights to your photos and notes. By posting a report, you give AgosAlert a free, non-exclusive permission to store it, resize the photo, and show it to everyone in the app and on this website, for as long as the report is up. Reports are deleted after 14 days. Remember that anyone who sees a report can screenshot it, and we can't take those copies back.

If something you see in AgosAlert is yours, or shows you, see Copyright and takedown.

8. Moderation

AgosAlert admins can mark reports as verified, remove reports, and suspend accounts that break these rules, with or without warning. We try to catch problems, but we can't check everything that's posted.
