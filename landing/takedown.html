Copyright and takedown

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Is your photo, your face, your plate number or your private information in an AgosAlert report? Email us and we'll take it down.
  • Your own report? Just delete it: the trash button on the post.

When to use this page

  • Someone posted your photo without your permission.
  • A photo shows you, your child, your plate number or the inside of your home, and you want it gone.
  • A report has your personal information (name, number, address) in its note.
  • A report is fake or breaks the rules for flood reports.

AgosAlert is a Philippine school project, so copyright here falls under the Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (RA 8293), and personal information under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173). The US "DMCA" doesn't apply, but the steps are similar: tell us, and we act on it.

How to ask

Email wildparchrome@gmail.com with the subject "AgosAlert takedown request", and include:

  1. Which report: the barangay, the poster's name and roughly when it was posted, or a screenshot.
  2. What's wrong: it's my photo / it shows me / it has my private information / it's fake.
  3. For a copied photo: that you own it (or act for the owner), and where you first posted it, if you did.
  4. Your name and how to reach you.
  5. A line saying the information you sent is true.

What happens next

  • We look at it and reply by email. If the problem is clear (a face, a plate number, private details), we remove the report first and ask questions later.
  • If we remove something, we tell the person who posted it, without passing on your contact details unless you say we can.
  • If the poster says the photo is really theirs, we'll ask you both for what you have and decide. While we sort it out, the report stays down.
  • Every report is deleted after 14 days anyway.

Please don't send false claims to get a real report removed. People may need it to stay safe.
